Who We Are

Founded in 1997, Fine Technology Solutions is everything an effective technology partner should be!

From first idea to finished product, we're with you every step of the way.

We have years of hands-on experience working with a wide range of clients--from home-based businesses to large enterprises.

As your preferred software developer, we work within your budget to get high quality professional results.

As your project partner, we plan, manage, and deliver technology projects with clear communication from kickoff to launch.

We help businesses adopt AI with confidence, building AI-assisted development and agentic workflows that automate routine work and speed up delivery.

Our commitment ensures a stable, secure computing environment, fast response times, and access to IT experts using a suite of world-class support tools.

As your technology partner, we are always ready to help with any size technology problem or device.


What we do

We create customized solutions tailored specifically for your business needs.

Software Solutions & Development

Our Agile development team brings experience and passion to make your software project a reality within your budget.

AI & Agentic Workflows

We help your business put AI to work. From AI-assisted development to agentic workflows that automate repetitive tasks, we design, build, and integrate solutions that save time and keep your team focused on what matters most.

Project Management

We keep your technology projects on time, on budget, and on track. From planning and scoping through delivery, our experienced project managers coordinate teams, manage risk, and keep you informed every step of the way.

FullStack Web Development

Our Full-Stack developers create comprehensive web solutions from front end to back end. We move fluidly across the stack making your ideas come to life.

Mobile Development

Our US-based Agile development team creates your app, bringing you both quality and value. Your mobile presence is more critical than ever, with native apps bringing more views and better conversion rates.

UI/UX Design

Put our deep understanding of Information Architecture to work in your website or mobile app. We pride ourselves on creating designs that allow your users focus on your content and brand.

Cloud Architecture

We design a cloud architecture tailored for your business. Whether it's on-premises or off-premises, we build seamless solutions to keep you connected and secure anywhere.

CyberSecurity

We evaluate your business --from infrastructure to policies to people-- and develop strategies and solutions to mitigate risk and protect against unauthorized access.

IT Infrastructure & Management

We design secure network infrastructure and keep it running, monitoring and maintaining it remotely and on-site so you can focus on your business.

On-Site & Remote Support

Serving San Diego County and Orange County. We show up when you need us. Our IT consultants also provide rapid assistance for many issues as long as you have a connection to the Internet.
